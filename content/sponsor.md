## Who We Are

TheHomeLab documents the real-world journey of running a self-hosted homelab — from bare-metal Proxmox clusters to Docker stacks, infrastructure-as-code, 3D printed lab gear, and everything in between.

We write detailed companion guides for every project so readers can follow along, reproduce the setup, and go deeper than a video allows.

---

## The Audience

Our readers are **technical homelab operators** — sysadmins, DevOps engineers, software developers, and self-hosting enthusiasts who run real infrastructure at home and want to learn from someone doing the same.

**Topics we cover:**

- Self-hosting & Docker
- Proxmox / virtualization
- Networking & security (VPN, DNS, reverse proxy)
- Infrastructure as Code (Komodo, Ansible, Git-based workflows)
- 3D printing for the lab (Bambu, OrcaSlicer, functional prints)
- Home automation (Home Assistant, Frigate, Zigbee)

This is an audience that **buys things** — NAS hardware, networking gear, filament, VPS plans, cloud tools, dev subscriptions. They research before they buy and trust technical creators over ads.
