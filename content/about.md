Hi, I'm David. My background is in acrobatics and live theater. I spent years working internationally, including a stint as Tarzan at Hong Kong Disneyland. These days my tinkering happens in a different arena.

My first real project was hacking an original Xbox with XBMC and gifting it to my grandfather as a replacement for his growing VCR collection. That about sums up what drives me: self-hosting, homelab infrastructure, 3D printing, building computers from scratch, and finding ways to make technology useful for the people around me.

I have a habit of breaking things while trying to improve them, occasionally figuring out how to fix them, and then somehow breaking them again. The second time is always an accident. Sharing what I build is what makes all of that worth it.

This site is where I document the process: what worked, what didn't, and what I'd do differently.
